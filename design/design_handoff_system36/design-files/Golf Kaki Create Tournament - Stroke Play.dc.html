Scoring formats explained
How each format scores your round.
Stroke play (medal)Selected
Count every stroke over the round — the lowest gross (or nett) total wins. The classic tournament format.
Stableford
Points scored per hole against par — birdie 3, par 2, bogey 1. Highest points win, and one bad hole never wrecks your round.
System 36
Auto-handicap format — build your handicap as you play, then score to par. Great for mixed-ability groups with no official index.

Tie-breaks explained
How a level score is settled.
CountbackSelected
Compares the tied players' scores over the last 9 holes. Still level? It steps down to the last 6, then last 3, then the 18th hole. The lowest score wins — no shared trophy.
Shared place
Tied players share the position and split any prize equally. The next place is skipped — two players tied for 1st means no outright 2nd.
